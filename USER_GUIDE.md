# Quick Reference: What Changed and How to Use

## Dashboard
**What Changed:**
- Cleaner, more modern design with left border accents on cards
- Better organized stats (Primary: Companies, Contacts, People, Brands; Secondary: Specialities, Main Specialties, Locations)
- Improved charts and recent activity lists
- Better hover effects and transitions

**How to Use:**
- Click any stat card to navigate to that section
- Hover over cards to see the enhanced visual feedback
- Charts are now more compact but still readable
- Recent activity items are clickable

## Contacts Page
**What Changed:**
- You can now click anywhere on a contact row to view it
- Removed the Eye icon button (no longer needed)
- Filter buttons now have icons for better clarity
- Table header is styled with bold text and background

**How to Use:**
- **View Contact**: Click anywhere on the row
- **Edit Contact**: Click the pencil icon button
- **Delete Contact**: Click the trash icon button
- **Filter**: Use the "All", "Person", or "Company" buttons
- **Search**: Type in the search box to filter results
- **Export**: Click "Export Excel" to download contacts

## Company Dialog - People Tab
**What Changed:**
- People cards now show contact information prominently
- You can add or edit contacts directly from the company dialog
- Better visual layout with separated sections

**How to Use:**
1. **Open Company**: Click on any company (or edit an existing one)
2. **Go to People Tab**: Click the "People" tab
3. **View Contact Info**: See email and mobile directly in the person card
4. **Add Contact to Person**:
   - Find the person without contact info
   - Click "Add Contact" button
   - Fill in the contact details
   - Save
5. **Edit Contact**:
   - Find the person with contact info
   - Click "Edit Contact" button
   - Update the contact details
   - Save
6. **Edit Person**: Click the pencil icon to edit person details
7. **Delete Person**: Click the trash icon to remove person

## Consistent Behavior Across Tables

All main tables now work the same way:
- **Companies**: Click row to view
- **Contacts**: Click row to view
- **People**: Click row to view
- **Brands**: Click row to view
- **Locations**: Click row to view

## Tips & Tricks

1. **Quick View**: Click any row to quickly view details without editing
2. **Edit Mode**: Use the pencil icon buttons to enter edit mode
3. **Keyboard Navigation**: Tab through buttons and forms as usual
4. **Mobile**: All improvements work on mobile devices
5. **Contact Management**: Add contacts to people directly from company editing - no need to navigate away!

## Common Workflows

### Workflow 1: Adding a New Company with People and Contacts
1. Go to Companies page
2. Click "Add Company"
3. Fill in company details
4. Save
5. Click on the company row to open it
6. Go to "People" tab
7. Click "Add Person"
8. Fill in person details
9. Save
10. Click "Add Contact" on the person card
11. Fill in contact details
12. Save
13. Done! Person now has contact info

### Workflow 2: Viewing Contact Details
1. Go to Contacts page
2. Filter by "Person" or "Company" if needed
3. Search for specific contact if needed
4. Click anywhere on the contact row
5. View all details in read-only mode
6. Close when done

### Workflow 3: Updating Contact for Existing Person
1. Go to Companies page
2. Click on the company
3. Go to "People" tab
4. Find the person
5. Click "Edit Contact" button
6. Update the details
7. Save
8. Contact info updates immediately
